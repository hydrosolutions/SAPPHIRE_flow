# Plan 343 — operational post-event verification and corrective-action review

## Status and scope

**DRAFT — not implementable.** This is high-risk scientific and user-visible behavior with scheduling, data retention and review API changes; the independent review requirements in `docs/workflow.md` apply. Plan 340 provides as-used forecast inputs, Plan 341 published-forecast history, and Plan 342 immutable threshold evaluations and attributed warning decisions. Plan 344 later adds the six-year cold archive; this plan must protect its new truth/review history from cleanup and use Plan 340's protected backup until then.

Existing `services/skill/metrics.py` and `services/skill/service.py` implement POD/FAR/CSI and lead-stratified hindcast skill. `flows/compute_skills.py` scores hindcasts, not the live CHWRR warning/release chain; its deployment has no routine cron. The architecture already defines versioned `skill_scores` and `SkillSource.OPERATIONAL`; extend/reuse that store for compatible continuous operational skill, while keeping distinct immutable warning-decision contingency generations and cases. Reuse tested formulas only where their sample definitions match. Do not relabel hindcast skill as operational verification or create a duplicate continuous-score source of truth.

This plan produces cases, scorecards and a small corrective-action ledger. It does not auto-change QC rules, thresholds, recipient lists, models, publish decisions, or warning text. It does not build a dashboard, CAP feed, or bulletin workflow. WMO-1364 in `docs/standards/wmo.md` supports forecast verification across accuracy/bias/reliability/resolution/sharpness; WMO-1150 supports separating threshold truth from warning relevance/impact; WMO-1072 and QMF-H support an auditable operational feedback loop. Do not claim that a threshold contingency table alone verifies social impact.

## Verification contract

- **Preliminary truth and timing.** Evaluate promptly from available quality-screened *preliminary* observations; finalization may take up to five years. Record exact observation IDs, values, QC status/flags, source revision, as-of cutoff, coverage and an independent maturity label `preliminary | final | unknown` in every verification generation. QC-passed does **not** mean final. If the source has no finality marker, remain preliminary/unknown until CHWRR records evidence-based final attestation; never infer finality from elapsed time alone. Distinguish `observation_missing`, `observation_suspect`, `not_evaluated`, `provisional_no_event` and `verified_final_no_event`; insufficient observation coverage is indeterminate, never a true negative. Later observation/QC corrections create a new report generation and case revision, preserving the prior result and linked decisions. Apply thresholds as they stood at forecast/evaluation time, with a separately labelled current-threshold sensitivity view if useful.
- **Versioned alignment contract.** Before T1/T2 scores are treated as operational, record the exact forecast valid-time convention (point or interval), observation aggregation/resampling and minimum QC coverage, allowed interpolation, ensemble-member/probability treatment, episode matching and separation, lead-bin edges, and the MVP metric set for each forecast type. Persist the alignment version and parameters in each result generation so a rule change cannot silently rewrite old scores. Use provisional, explicitly labelled settings for 2027 shadow data and let CHWRR confirm event-separation and material-error criteria after real cases are reviewed, including during the November 2026 visit.
- **Historical observation changes have more than one path.** The present DHM adapter and flow advance by a latest-timestamp watermark, so scoring reruns alone cannot see corrected or deleted older source records. Implement one real operator-controlled historical reimport boundary for supplied corrected series; where CHWRR/DHM supplies a historical revision feed/export, add bounded fetch/diff across the provisional five-year window, including tombstones/deletions and QC changes. Record the before/after value, curve/QC version, source and affected-window enqueue atomically with the current projection change. The reimport/reconciliation path and ordinary ingestion must share station write exclusion: use the `observation_write:{station_id}` Prefect control from `docs/standards/orchestration.md` for flows and one transaction-scoped PostgreSQL advisory-lock helper keyed by tenant and station for *every* writer, including ordinary ingest, CLI reimport and the later Flow 12 producer. A whole-station lock deliberately serializes overlapping and disjoint periods for that station; hold it through revision, projection and outbox commit. The native Flow 12 rating-curve reprocessing producer is **not** part of this plan: remaining Plan 035 work and a shipped Flow 12 must later use this same revision/outbox contract and receive their own end-to-end test. Do not claim native curve-triggered rescore before then. If CHWRR never updates the historical series and no revision source exists, report the last checked source/coverage and keep truth maturity `preliminary | unknown`; unchanged data or elapsed time does not establish finality. Keep historical checks separate from the low-latency ingest watermark.
- **Automatic versus human.** Compute separate contingency tables for (1) automatic forecast threshold candidates and (2) published human warning decisions against the same observed event catalog. Within automatic candidates, report raw strategy results and the selected-published-forecast recheck as distinct cohorts; do not blend their denominators or attribute a pooled raw alert to the chosen forecast. For a human warning, its availability starts at actual publication time and ends at replacement, withdrawal or expiry; calculate decision lead to observed onset from that publication time, retaining source-forecast issue lead separately. A warning first published after observed onset is a late response, never a predictive hit at the source forecast's earlier lead. Show hits, misses, false alarms and correct negatives with POD, FAR and CSI by station, parameter, danger level and decision/forecast lead bins as appropriate, with denominators, observation coverage and missing-truth counts. Publish preliminary and final scorecards as separate generations with prominent maturity labels; never blend their denominators or present a preliminary score as settled skill. A published observation-sourced warning is reported separately from forecast prediction skill; it can still have a decision/latency outcome.
- **Event catalog and forecast quality.** Build observed high/low-flow episodes independently of alerts, so an event with no forecast or no candidate becomes a miss/coverage case. Record observed onset/peak/end and match to forecast valid windows by a documented station/level episode separation rule. Show peak magnitude and timing error, bias/MAE (and existing ensemble scores where meaningful) by station/lead for *all* operational forecasts and separately for the human-published subset, including forecasts that never crossed a warning threshold. Label the event-level denominator separately from issue/lead sample counts to avoid counting one flood repeatedly as many independent events.
- **Dismissal outcomes.** Compare each DISMISSED candidate to observed exceedance and a separate CHWRR relevance judgment: `correct_dismissal` (no event), `wrong_dismissal` (event and relevant), `true_but_not_relevant` (event occurred but threshold/recipient/context was unsuitable), or `indeterminate` (missing/ambiguous truth). Label each classification preliminary or final. Do not infer relevance solely from threshold crossing; retain the hydrologist's original reason and later adjudicator, time and note. Also flag published false alarms and unpublished event misses, with provisional wording until truth is final. If revised observations change a case, reopen it for CHWRR review and retain the previous classification and action history.
- **Cadence and effort.** Run daily after a configurable *preliminary-observation availability* lag, not a finalization delay. Re-score affected periods whenever supplied source/QC revisions or historical reimports arrive, including changes in year five; a future native Flow 12 producer may use the same path after Plan 035 delivers it. Keep an authorized bounded manual rerun. During 2027 testing/shadow operation, CHWRR hydrologists inspect every provisional missed high-severity event, wrong dismissal, material timing/magnitude error and published false alarm within one working day; CHWRR and hydrosolutions discuss exception trends weekly and sample correct dismissals. For routine operation, keep daily preliminary scoring, review material exceptions promptly, and review aggregate station/lead trends monthly. On evidenced finalization, produce a final generation and reconcile it against earlier findings. These cadences are proposed operating defaults; CHWRR management confirms the duty roster and event criteria after inspecting real data. Offer the event-separation/material-error discussion during the November 2026 Kathmandu visit.
- **Actions and ownership.** Every material case may link a typed action: `qc_rule`, `threshold_or_recipient`, `model_retrain`, `model_deactivate`, `observation_source`, or `no_change`, with owner, rationale, status and evidence link. CHWRR owns finality attestation where the source provides none, event/relevance adjudication, publication practice, threshold/recipient approval and model activation/deactivation decisions. Hydrosolutions diagnoses model/technical causes and proposes QC/model changes; CHWRR accepts operational changes. Changes follow their own reviewed implementation/configuration workflow, and verification records the before/after version rather than silently editing configuration.

## Tasks

### T1 — Deterministic observed-event and verification datasets

**Outcome.** A versioned run creates reproducible preliminary or final observed episodes and forecast/evaluation matches, including no-forecast and missing-truth cases.

**In / Out.** In: typed event/case and run schema/store, observation/QC cutoff, source revision and independent finality evidence, immutable result snapshot, matching service, station/parameter/level scope and explicit time windows. The existing `observation_versions` table does not cover every upstream historical correction, so capture every as-used truth revision needed for comparison. Add an operator-controlled, tenant/station-scoped historical reimport producer for a supplied corrected series, with authorized `WritePrincipal` and audit attribution, plus bounded source correction/deletion fetch when upstream access exists. Ordinary ingest and reimport share the station-scoped `observation_write:{station_id}` Prefect control and the same transaction-scoped tenant/station PostgreSQL advisory-lock helper; commit old revision, new current projection and affected-window outbox event atomically while holding that DB lock. Apply role-independent append-only mutation guards and insert-only grants to report generations, truth snapshots, case adjudications and action history; protect them from cleanup and include them in Plan 340's protected backup until Plan 344. Audit missing revision access and distinguish "no update supplied" from "checked, unchanged". Out: retrospective scores without evidence, automatic QC correction, age-based finality inference, a claim that CHWRR will update historical data, and native Flow 12 integration before it exists. Do not let current thresholds or upserted observations overwrite earlier generations.

**Verification.** `uv run pytest tests/unit/services/verification/test_matching.py tests/integration/store/test_verification_store.py`; synthetic flood, low-flow episode, failed forecast cycle, preliminary observation later corrected or deleted in year five through the real historical reimport boundary and finalized, missing QC/partial coverage, threshold changed later, and event across issue cycles. Test competing ordinary-ingest and CLI-reimport writers using the shared advisory-lock helper and an interrupted transaction: old revision, current projection and affected-window event commit together or not at all. Assert idempotent unchanged rerun, no age-based finality without supplied evidence, mutation denial for historical tables and no cleanup of linked truth/cases. Do not inject a fake native Flow 12 event as end-to-end proof.

**Pre-change.** RED: no operational observed-event catalog or per-run evaluation matching exists; hindcast skill cannot classify a human dismissal.

### T2 — Separate automatic, published and continuous-forecast scores

**Outcome.** Each maturity-labelled report contains two distinct warning contingency tables plus continuous timing/magnitude diagnostics for all operational forecasts, stratified by station/level/lead with explicit sample counts.

**In / Out.** In: verification service and warning-decision score schema, compatible continuous metrics in versioned `skill_scores` with `SkillSource.OPERATIONAL`, reuse of applicable `services/skill/metrics.py` formulas, deterministic lead-bin and episode rules. Add a typed operational cohort (`all_operational` or `human_published`) to score and generation identity, natural-key uniqueness, latest-generation/read predicates and API filters; select the published cohort by forecast ID and publication history, not model ID alone. The existing `skill_scores`/`skill_generations` scope has no cohort dimension, so merely reusing `SkillSource.OPERATIONAL` would collide or supersede one cohort. Keep older rows and previous-image reads valid during expand/contract migration, with an explicit legacy-unscoped value rather than silently treating old scores as either new cohort. Preserve observed-revision/report-generation references so a preliminary score can later be superseded without erasing its historical interpretation. Out: duplicate continuous-score table, automatic model retraining or optimization of warning thresholds from the same small test period. Never treat `indeterminate` as correct negative or divide by zero without an explicit undefined score.

**Verification.** `uv run pytest tests/unit/services/verification/test_scores.py`; table with known POD/FAR/CSI, separate raw/selected-forecast/human results when the chosen model disagrees with pooled alert and after a wrong dismissal, publication after observed onset, replacement/withdrawal/expiry before onset, source-forecast lead versus actual warning-decision lead, no-alert timing error, lead-bin boundaries, low-flow cases, undefined denominator, missing/partial observations, and separate preliminary versus final score generations. Assert simultaneous all-operational and human-published score generations with the same station/model/lead/metric remain distinct in uniqueness, latest reads and API output, including a prior-image compatibility read. Change one alignment parameter and its version, generate new scores, and prove the prior generation remains unchanged and queryable.

**Pre-change.** RED: operational alert and publication decisions are not input to the existing hindcast skill flow; no human-decision scorecard exists.

### T3 — Daily/on-demand verification and review API

**Outcome.** A registered daily run creates an idempotent preliminary report after the observation availability lag; revised observations trigger historical re-score, and authorized CHWRR reviewers can inspect generations, adjudicate relevance, and link corrective actions.

**In / Out.** In: Prefect flow/registration/config, revision-triggered affected-window reruns plus periodic five-year source reconciliation when the source supports historical access, reviewer read/write API and an audited CHWRR finality-attestation action when the source provides no marker, scoped consumer exclusion, append-only audit for adjudication/action changes, least-privilege DB grants, logging and failure health signal. Add station-scoped `verification_adjudicate` and separately granted `finality_attest` local user permissions; a `review` or `publish` grant alone cannot write these records. Propose daily verification at `40 2 * * *` UTC on the current `default` pool with concurrency 1, gated by a CHWRR-set preliminary-availability lag before activation. Where historical source access exists, propose weekly reconciliation at `10 3 * * 0` UTC on `default`, concurrency 1, at most 60 pages of 31 station-days per run; retain a cursor until the five-year sweep completes. Add a named `verification_run` shared concurrency limit of 1 across daily and weekly deployments so the schedules cannot overlap even if one run lasts beyond the nominal offset. Share the DB bulk-write slot with forecast/recheck operations for conflicting writes. Register matching CLI/Compose cron defaults and benchmark source/API, CPU, memory, DB and queue latency with overlapping forecast/recheck work before activation; change the pool or schedule if the measured limit fails. A missed schedule, stale preliminary run or incomplete reconciliation sweep emits a pipeline-health failure/stale signal. Out: notifications to recipients, direct mutation of model/QC/threshold settings, dashboard UI. A failed run leaves prior reports intact and visible as stale; no silent success on partial station coverage or historical reconciliation failure. Avoid recomputing the entire five-year history daily when no source revision changed.

**Verification.** `uv run pytest tests/unit/flows/test_verify_operations.py tests/unit/api/test_verification_api.py tests/integration/store/test_verification_store.py`; inspect both CLI-registered and Compose-effective schedules/work pools, deterministic clock/cutoff, retry, shared daily/weekly concurrency with a deliberately long run, forecast/recheck overlap load and DB-write serialization, bounded/cursor-resumed five-year sweep, late revision in year five through reimport, source deletion, station scope, read-only/revoked/cross-station denial for adjudication and finality attestation, actor attribution and evidence for finality attestation, reopened case, report pagination, and failure/stale behavior.

**Pre-change.** RED: no scheduled operational verification deployment or case/adjudication API exists.
